# Opportunity and Experiment Policy

## Objective

Preserve and evaluate every legal, potentially performance-improving tactic without presenting speculation as proof or exposing production accounts to uncontrolled risk.

The registry is not a pilot connector list or release gate. The approved Pilot Scope Record controls implementation.

## Opportunity states

- `discovered`: captured but not yet assessed.
- `assessed`: evidence, legality, platform status, effort, and risk recorded.
- `eligible`: may be proposed for a controlled experiment.
- `blocked_prerequisite`: potentially eligible after data, permission, capability, or policy requirements are met.
- `testing`: part of an active approved experiment.
- `supported`: reproduced with adequate evidence for defined conditions.
- `inconclusive`: completed without sufficient evidence.
- `rejected`: mechanism cannot be used; record the legitimate objective and alternatives.
- `retired`: previously useful but obsolete or no longer permitted.

## Required fields

- Title and underlying business objective.
- Source and evidence tier.
- Applicable funnel stage, platform, audience, and business type.
- Expected upside and mechanism.
- Legal assessment status and owner.
- Platform-policy and API-capability status.
- Data, consent, creative, account, and integration prerequisites.
- Estimated effort, cost, time to signal, and reversibility.
- Brand, account, privacy, security, and financial risk.
- Proposed hypothesis and test design.
- Decision owner and review date.
- Safer or more compliant alternatives.

## Eligibility rules

An opportunity may be tested when:

- no known law or explicit authorization boundary prohibits the mechanism;
- current platform rules and account permissions permit the proposed execution;
- required data rights and consent are documented;
- the experiment has a bounded population, budget, duration, and stop condition;
- measurement can distinguish meaningful business outcomes;
- the pilot can link the result to the selected CRM's qualified outcomes and booked revenue when the claim requires it;
- an authorized user approves the risk and test plan.

Legal uncertainty is not interpreted as permission. The system records the uncertainty and routes it to a qualified human owner.

## Evidence tiers

1. Official capability or policy evidence.
2. Reproduced internal causal evidence.
3. Credible external study or reproducible practice.
4. Plausible architectural or marketing hypothesis.
5. Promotional anecdote or unsupported claim.

Only tiers 1–2 can support broad autonomous use. Lower tiers can still justify controlled experiments.

## Experiment contract

Every experiment must define:

- hypothesis;
- eligible population and exclusions;
- control and treatment;
- primary business metric;
- guardrail metrics;
- attribution method;
- minimum sample or decision rule;
- maximum budget and duration;
- early stopping conditions;
- novelty and interference risks;
- owner and approver;
- post-test decision options.

## Prohibited execution patterns

- Illegal activity.
- Unauthorized access or data acquisition.
- Circumventing platform enforcement, access controls, or account restrictions.
- Fake identities, synthetic engagement, undisclosed impersonation, or fabricated testimonials.
- Uploading audiences without documented rights and eligibility.
- Sending suppressed or prohibited communications.
- Hiding material limitations or presenting invented results.
- Allowing an agent to change its own authorization policy.

## Promotion to routine use

An experiment becomes a reusable tactic only when the evidence states where it worked, the effect is practically meaningful, guardrails stayed within bounds, data quality was adequate, and the procedure has an owner, skill version, monitoring, and rollback path. Promotion does not automatically grant autonomy.

For AI Reach work, evaluation also covers evidence accuracy, factual accuracy, approval acceptance, execution correctness, and later CRM outcomes.

Controlled answer presence, citation, or referral changes remain observational unless a valid design supports a stronger claim.
