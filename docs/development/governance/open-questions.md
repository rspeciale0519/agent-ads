# Open Questions

These questions do not alter the accepted product scope, but they must be resolved at the indicated decision point.

## Pilot business — before implementation planning is finalized

- What business, offer, geography, sales cycle, capacity, margins, and primary qualified outcome will be used?
- Which paid and organic accounts already exist, and who owns them?
- Which platforms currently carry spend or meaningful historical data?
- Which CRM, analytics, commerce, call-tracking, booking, and billing systems hold outcomes?
- What volume is available to evaluate performance?
- Which jurisdictions, regulated topics, or customer-data constraints apply?
- Who approves campaigns, budgets, content, data use, and incidents?

## Platform access — Phase 0

- What developer apps, tokens, reviews, certifications, contracts, or account tiers are required for each paid and organic connector at implementation time?
- Which test/sandbox accounts can be used?
- Which organic channels should use native versus Postiz/Blotato routes initially?
- Which exact objective, format, targeting, analytics, comment, edit, and deletion capabilities are required per platform?
- Are Pinterest, Snapchat, or Amazon Ads desired in a later committed release?

## Product and UX — before detailed interface design

- Is the primary operating model direct client use, agency-managed service, or both at launch?
- Is white labeling required for MVP?
- Which notification channels are required beyond in-app and email?
- Are mobile-native apps required, or is responsive approval sufficient?
- What level of raw agent-run visibility should clients versus operators receive?
- Which languages/locales are required for the pilot?

## Architecture and vendors — before Phase 1

- Selected cloud and managed-service preferences.
- Identity provider.
- Temporal Cloud versus self-managed/equivalent.
- Object storage and observability backend.
- Hermes deployment and model-provider policy.
- Secret manager and key management.
- Postiz, Blotato, native APIs, or mixed organic route.
- Creative generation/rendering vendors.

## Data and measurement — before Phase 3 exit

- Canonical qualified outcome and economics.
- Attribution views and decision uses.
- Identity resolution and consent boundaries.
- Historical backfill depth.
- Data freshness and reconciliation tolerances.
- Retention, export, deletion, and audit periods.
- Whether PostgreSQL is sufficient for pilot analytical volume.

## Autonomy and risk — before pilot mutation

- Initial budget and action caps.
- Required step-up authentication actions.
- Always-human actions beyond the documented defaults.
- Incident notification and kill-switch owners.
- Legal/platform-policy review owners.
- Evidence threshold for any first bounded-autonomy action.

## Commercialization — before multi-client release

- Pricing and usage metering.
- Client data ownership and contractual responsibilities.
- Agency delegation and support model.
- Platform developer terms for managing third-party client accounts.
- White-label, billing, support, and SLA requirements.
- Data residency and regional deployment needs.
