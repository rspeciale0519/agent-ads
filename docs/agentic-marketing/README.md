# Agentic Marketing Research Context

> **Implementation authority:** The research remains the evidence base. Product decisions made after the audit—including Hermes-centered multi-agent orchestration, seven paid-advertising connectors, and seven organic-publishing connectors in the MVP—are specified in [`docs/development`](../development/README.md) and supersede narrower research-era MVP recommendations.

This directory is the permanent project context produced from the exhaustive review of the `Marketing` X bookmark category completed on 2026-08-06.

## What was reviewed

- 75 unique bookmarked X posts, confirmed by two independent full-feed passes after an initial fast pass missed virtualized items.
- Every top-level post body, quote, note tweet, and embedded X Article available through the signed-in browser session.
- 13 unique long-form X Articles in full.
- Five high-value linked YouTube episodes through their complete descriptions, chapter maps, examples, and authored section summaries.
- Thread replies for the most consequential implementation claims, including the Facebook Ads agent, bulk creative uploader, warehouse loop, Perplexity Computer demonstration, and Higgsfield workflow.
- Current official documentation for platform APIs, advertising rules, data use, agent security, search spam, direct marketing, and the principal implementation tools.

## Research boundaries

The work was read-only. No post, reply, like, follow, bookmark, share, form, lead magnet, or X account state was changed.

The source set contains promotional claims, lead-generation posts, and tactics that conflict with platform rules. This documentation does not treat engagement or repetition as validation. Claims are labeled as verified, plausible, unsupported, or rejected. A tactic's presence in the source index is not a recommendation to implement it.

## Media limitations

- YouTube exposed autogenerated transcript tracks for the five relevant episodes, but its transcript endpoint returned a failed precondition or empty response during the research session. The full descriptions, chapter maps, worked examples, and section summaries were reviewed; they are not represented here as verbatim transcripts.
- Several X videos had no caption track or transcript. Their post copy, visible frames, related long-form article/episode, and thread context were reviewed where available. These are marked `limited`, not silently counted as transcript-complete.
- Two lead magnets required posting a keyword or submitting a form. They were not accessed because the approved goal prohibited X mutations and did not authorize form submission.
